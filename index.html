Norah's Kitchen: reservation chatbot

A fictional restaurant with a real working booking system

Norah's Kitchen is my first AI chatbot project, built from an interest in exploring how AI agents can create practical value for businesses. I designed a custom restaurant reservation interface and connected it to n8n workflows, Google Gemini and Google Sheets to demonstrate how AI can streamline customer interactions and reservation management.

The chatbot was first built in n8n, where the workflow became the blueprint for the live version that runs today.

A guest visits the restaurant website, taps Reserve a Table, and starts a conversation with the chatbot. It collects the guest's name, preferred date, time, number of guests and phone number, asking for any missing details along the way.

Book a table

Checks the date is in the future, holds the table for 15 minutes and confirms with a booking number.

Change a booking

Updates the original booking, for example 7pm to 8pm, with no duplicate.

Cancel and look up

Cancels but keeps the record. Guests can look up their details and rebook.

Answer questions

Built to Solve a Real Problem

The chatbot automates manual booking work so restaurant staff can dedicate their time to higher-priority tasks throughout the day.

  • No missed bookings. Guests can book any time, even after hours or when the phone is busy. A missed call is a missed table.
  • Less time on the phone. Staff are not stuck taking bookings, repeating hours and directions, or writing details down by hand.
  • Fewer mistakes. The chatbot asks for everything up front, checks the sheet, and gives the guest a booking number, so no double bookings, no missing phone numbers, no guessing.
  • Changes and cancellations handled. Guests can change or cancel by chat, and the same row is updated, so staff do not have to hunt through a book to find it.
  • Staff free for the guests in front of them. Less paperwork and phone time means more time serving the people already in the restaurant.
  • Everything in one place. Every booking sits in one sheet the whole team can see, instead of notes, messages, and phone calls in different places.
